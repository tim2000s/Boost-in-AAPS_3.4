## What this measures (and what it can't)

No glucodynamic model exists, so counterfactual BG under a higher cap is **unobservable**. This replays the *policy* over real telemetry: triggers from actual fields, raise/revert dynamics judged against **actual** forward-BG, added insulin **priced** against observed lows. The trustworthy outputs are the **revert frequency** and the **safe-slice size**; the TIR buy-back is an upper bound and is not claimed here.

### Column key
- **n_state** — CONFIRMED cycles. **n_clip** — of those, where fd hit confirmedCap (cap binding).
- **n_safe_clip** — of those, the ones in the low-IOB safe slice (iob < 5% TDD, BG≥140). *If n_safe_clip ≪ n_clip, most cap-clips are high-IOB — the recovering-highs rejection, seen per-user.*
- **n_qual** — safe clips that ALSO had a sustained high follow and retrospective need (the full trigger).
- **raises / reverts** — policy actions once armed. **safe_prelow_pct** — empirical fraction of the safe slice that went low within 3h = the two-test price the extra insulin is charged at. **priced_low_U** — extra units × that rate.

A **high revert share** (≥33% of cap-changes reverted) means it churns caps at the cost of lows — NO-GO on churn.
