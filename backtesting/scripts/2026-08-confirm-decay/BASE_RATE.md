# Is the post-confirm low rate elevated?


Each confirm compared against control windows from the same participant, matched on hour of day to within an hour, starting glucose to within 15 mg/dL and insulin on board to within 0.5 U, with no confirm in the window or the hour before it. Outcome is the lowest glucose in the following 180 minutes.


1074 confirms matched, 11 participants, median 43 controls each.


## below 70


After a confirm 22.8 per cent, in matched control windows 14.0 per cent. Difference +8.8 points [+3.9, +14.0]: confirms are WORSE.


## below 54


After a confirm 7.6 per cent, in matched control windows 1.9 per cent. Difference +5.7 points [+3.0, +8.5]: confirms are WORSE.
