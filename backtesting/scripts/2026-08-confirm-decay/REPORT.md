# Confirming into a decaying acceleration


Every entry into CONFIRMED on the V5/V6 engine, classified by what acceleration did over the 4 cycles before it, and followed for 180 minutes. A confirm counts as decaying when acceleration fell at least 10 points from its peak in the approach and fell monotonically from that peak.


1268 confirms from 12 participants, 2026-06-26 to 2026-08-12.


## Does it predict a low


The comparison is the point. A crash rate for decaying confirms on its own says nothing, because some confirms are followed by lows whatever the approach looked like.


| approach | confirms | participants | went below 70 | below 54 | median nadir | median dose |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| decaying | 742 | 12 | 24.0% [16.5, 32.8] | 8.6% [4.7, 13.4] | 92 | 1.25 U |
| sustained or rising | 526 | 12 | 22.1% [14.2, 29.6] | 7.8% [4.9, 11.2] | 99 | 1.60 U |

Difference in the rate of going below 70: +1.9 points [-4.1, +8.1]. not distinguishable.


## How much insulin the two approaches attract


If the damper is to be worth building, the decaying case has to be receiving enough insulin for damping it to matter.


| approach | median dose | 90th percentile | median action multiplier | median IOB at confirm |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| decaying | 1.25 U | 3.20 U | 1.80 | 1.07 U |
| sustained or rising | 1.60 U | 3.50 U | 1.80 | 1.19 U |
