# OpenAPS Data Commons: announcement size in the three meal windows


126,064 announcements from 181 participants across 3 platform groups. Breakfast 06:00-09:00, lunch 12:00-15:00, dinner 17:00-20:00.


The Commons carries no carbohydrate entries, only carbohydrate on board, so an announcement here is a step increase in COB of 8 g or more between adjacent cycles. That measures the amount entered less whatever decayed inside the cycle, which biases every size slightly low, and merges entries made inside one cycle. It is a different measurement from the recorded entries in the Jaeb corpora and the two should not be compared to the gram.
